About The Club

More Than a Trip, It's a Travel Family

Paramount Tourism Club brings together people who love to explore. We plan comfortable, safe and affordable tours so you can focus on what matters: the views, the food, the culture and the friendships made along the way.

From weekend getaways to multi-day northern expeditions, every journey is organized with care, from transport and stays to guided sightseeing.
